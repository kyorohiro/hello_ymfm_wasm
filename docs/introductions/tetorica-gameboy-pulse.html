1. Change one value and listen

Duty is the proportion of each cycle occupied by the pulse. Keep pitch at A4 and volume at 10, changing only the width. The 25% and 75% waveforms differ in polarity/phase, but sound nearly identical on their own.

No sound plays until you press Play.

This prototype emulates the DMG digital audio. It does not reproduce the hardware amplifier/speaker tone or analog high-pass filter. Only the preview applies a fixed gain and 10 ms fades at both ends; the plot shows unprocessed PCM.

3. Where does the hardware create sound?

The hardware described here is the original Game Boy (DMG); the board references cover DMG-CPU-06. The APU is inside the same IC as the CPU, unlike the Mega Drive with its separate YM2612 chip.

Only Pulse 1 has hardware pitch sweep; Pulse 2 has the same four duty choices. Wave uses 32-point waveform memory, while Noise is a separate circuit driven by a pseudorandom sequence.

For physical component locations, see Gekkio's DMG-CPU-06 board traces and schematics. These materials are licensed CC BY 4.0; no images are reproduced here. Do not generalize component locations to other DMG revisions or the Game Boy Color.
