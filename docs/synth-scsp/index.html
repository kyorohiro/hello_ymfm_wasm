SCSP Synth

Sega Saturn · 32-slot PCM sound chip · experimental

Start audio, then play the keys below or use A W S E D F T G Y H U J K.

Slot 1 is the source voice. Keyboard uses 31 free slots; the oldest voice is stolen when full. PCM and envelope edits affect new notes. Pitch and mix can change held notes.

PCM drawing, audio conversion and preset files are software features. Forward-loop PCM and hardware envelope codes are supported. LFO, slot FM, DSP editing and SSF playback are not exposed here.
